# Yakimma — real website starter

**Tagline:** The world you can show off  
**Style:** Pink + teal

This is a runnable first backend/frontend starter—not a published website yet. It supports real local sign-up/login, password hashing, database-backed posts and balances, video/photo uploads, likes, the MC-to-LC exchange, shop purchases, themes, real friends, groups, secret codes, and a Nail Salon.

## What works in this starter

- Sign up and sign in with a username and password. Signing up also asks for your religion and country, which show on your profile for everyone to see. Accounts made before that are asked to choose them once, the next time they open Yakimma.
- Passwords are hashed before they are saved.
- Posts and likes are stored in SQLite.
- Upload an existing image or video (the site does not record videos).
- Every *new unique user's first like on a post* rewards the post owner 10 MC. Removing and re-adding the same like does not farm another reward.
- Exchange 1,000 MC for 1 LC.
- Shop prices: Nails 1 LC, Speed 40 MC, Effects 70 MC, Goth Theme 10 MC, Pastel Theme 20 MC.
- Buy items; purchases and balances are stored in the database.
- **Themes:** pick a theme in Settings. Pink & Teal, Rose Pink and Mint Teal are free. Goth (mostly black) and Pastel (rainbow pastels) unlock in the Shop and switch on as soon as you buy them. A signed-in person's theme is saved to their account.
- **Groups:** browse groups people have made, search them, join and leave. Anyone signed in can start up to 5 groups, and only the person who made a group can delete it (its posts and chat are deleted with it). Open a group you're in to chat with the other members or share posts (text, photo or video) that only members can see and like.
- **Codes:** enter secret codes to win MC, LC, a ginger cat nail sticker, or a key to the secret group Cuties. Each account can use each code once, and too many wrong guesses means a 15-minute wait. The codes and their prizes are in the `codes` list near the top of `server.js`.
- **Profile pictures:** pick an emoji avatar and background colour, or upload a photo and drag/zoom it to fit the circle. Photos are resized to 400×400 in the browser (which also strips hidden data such as location) and checked on the server to be real images.
- **Seeing a profile picture big 🔍:** tap the big picture on anyone's profile (or your own) and it shows on 70% of the screen. Zoom in or out (50% to 400%) with **+** and **−**, the mouse wheel or by pinching, drag it around while zoomed in, and tap **100%** to go back. Your own has **🎨 Change picture** in it too. Tap outside it, ✕ or Escape to close it.
- **Friends:** send, accept, decline and cancel friend requests between real accounts (decline on the Friends page or on their profile), and remove friends. Friends can chat privately; unread messages show as a number on the Friends button.
- **Chats** check for new messages every 3 seconds while open. Messages can be up to 500 characters, and sending more than 30 a minute means a short wait.
- **Message actions:** tap a message to react with any of 1,898 emojis (Unicode Emoji 15.1, from `public/emoji-data.json`), forward it to a friend or group, or edit or delete your own. Deleted messages show as "This message was deleted". The person who made a group can delete anyone's messages in it. Emojis use the device's own emoji font, so they're Apple emojis on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- **Blocking:** block someone from a chat message, a post, a friend request or your friends list. You won't see their messages or posts, they can't send you friend requests, and any friendship ends. Unblock them in the Blocked list on the Friends page.
- **Nail Salon:** locked until you buy Nails (1 LC). Pick your hand's skin tone, then choose a friend and give them a manicure: shape, length, polish colour, finish (glossy, matte, shimmer, chrome) and as much nail art per nail as you like. Each piece has its own colour, and you can drag it around on a big close-up of the nail, or size it, turn it and move it with sliders. Polish and art colours can be picked from a colour wheel too. Finished sets are saved and show on the friend's profile.
- **18+ zone:** in Settings, **Turn on 18+?** takes four steps: confirm you're 18+, enter your email, type the code sent to it, and pass a face scan. In the zone the site turns dark and scary, and you only see 18+ posts and groups. Shop, Codes, About The Creator and Advertisement aren't in the zone. You can only chat with people who are 18+, and nobody outside the zone sees any of it. **Kuchisake-onna** takes the place of Nails, and the 👹 button at the top turns the scary sounds off. Make her scream 5 times and Hachishakusama, a very tall woman in a white hat, starts stalking you around the zone with warnings and a girl's quiet "po… po… po…". To make her leave, turn 18+ off and take 3 photos of nails with 📷 on the normal site. Entering the zone gives you the 🩸 Blood theme for free. The zone has its own theme (Blood to start with), which you can change in Settings, and turning 18+ off brings back your normal theme. Turn 18+ off in Settings to go back. On Yakimma running on your own computer (localhost), the accounts Bleh and Manhatukfayay can skip the face scan by typing its username and password. The list of accounts is `FACE_SCAN_SKIPPERS` in `server.js`, and skipping never works on the live site.
- **Kiddy zone 🧸:** in Settings, **Turn on the kiddy zone?** is for kids, and a parent sets it up in six steps: confirm, type the parent's email, enter the code sent to it, choose a parent-only password, scan their face (they can skip the face scan, and save their face later in Settings), and fill in the child's and the parent's birth dates. The birth dates have to be filled in to turn the zone on, and they're never shared: nobody sees them, not even on the account's own pages. In the kiddy zone you only see posts made there, you can only add and talk to friends who are in it too, and you only have Feed, Profile, Settings, Friends, Nails, Learn and the Shop. Only the parent password turns it off (Settings asks for it), and **Forgot the parent password?** emails a new code to the same parent so they can choose a new one. Signing out of the kiddy zone needs the parent password, or a face scan that matches the parent's face if one is saved; the top account menu has no Sign out in the kiddy zone, so it's done from the Profile page. Yakimma keeps 128 numbers that describe the face, never a picture. A parent can save or change the face in Settings with the parent password. Once a parent has set it up, turning it back on is one tap (accounts set up before the birth dates were asked for fill them in first). You can't be in the 18+ zone and the kiddy zone at the same time.
- **Learn! 📚:** a quiz game in the kiddy zone (it only shows there) with Math, English, Science, Islam and Christianity, 10 questions a round. For each subject a parent types the parent password to choose the class (Class 1 to 9), then picks Asia or Western and confirms with the parent password again; next time the quiz starts straight away. Asia is harder and Western is easier in every subject: about half of each quiz comes from a level up for Asia and a level down for Western (for Math, from Class 3 up). Islam only opens for accounts whose religion is Islam, and Christianity only for accounts whose religion is Christianity. Islam and Christianity each first need a parent to verify, with the parent password or a scan of the parent's face (if one is saved). Math questions are made up fresh for each class (Class 1 Asia is times tables 1 to 10 and hard sums; Class 1 Western is odd and even, simple sums and counting from 1 to 120; Class 2 Asia is times tables 3 to 15, 3-digit sums and multiplying, money in taka and poisha, meters and centimeters, and clocks; Class 2 Western is hundreds, tens and ones, money in dollars and pennies, and clocks, read with the 5 times table); English, Science, Islam and Christianity questions are in `public/learn-questions.json` (Class 1–3, 4–6 and 7–9), where you can add more.
- **Deleting and reporting posts:** you can delete your own posts (videos, photos and text) with 🗑️ Delete, and report other people's with 🚩 Report. Reporting for **❓ Something else** opens a box under it to say what's wrong in your own words. What you write goes to the creator as a 🔔 notification (tap it to open the Reports page), and shows with the report on the Reports page. Comments can be reported too (🚩 Report under someone else's comment). Nobody can report the creator's posts or comments, or block the creator: there's no 🚩 Report or 🚫 Block button on them, and Yakimma says no if anyone tries. The Reports page shows who sent each report. The admins, Bleh and Creator (`ADMINS` in `server.js`), always have 🛠️ as their profile picture, which nobody else can pick (except Creator, whose picture is always the Pastel Yakimma Cat Badge: `FIXED_AVATARS`), and can delete anyone's groups and anyone's messages in group chats, can switch into the 18+ zone or the kiddy zone and back straight away (no checks, parent password or parent's face, so they can see what's wrong), and see reported posts and comments on the 🚩 Reports page. Only the creator can do anything there (suspend, delete it, or keep it); the other admins can only look. Only the creator can delete other people's posts, anywhere on Yakimma. The Reports page isn't in the kiddy zone: posts reported in the kiddy zone show on the normal site's Reports page, and the 18+ zone's Reports page has the 18+ ones.
- **Display names 🪪:** admins can also have a display name (up to 30 characters, spaces and emojis allowed), set with **🪪 Display name** on their Profile. Everyone sees it first, with their @username after it, and people can search for them by either. Regular accounts can't have one.
- **The creator:** the creator's username is **Creator** and their display name is **Manhatukfayay**. Nobody else can have "creator" or "manhatukfayay" anywhere in their username or display name.
- **Profiles:** tap anyone's picture or name (on posts, in chats, in group member lists and on the Friends page) to see their profile. Yours opens your own Profile page. On a smaller window (1050px or less), the things on someone's profile (MC, LC, items, friends, country, religion, followers) shrink to just their emoji and number; hover over one to see what it is.
- **Video thumbnails 🖼️:** when you upload a video, a moment from it is picked as its thumbnail (the picture shown before it plays). Move the slider and tap **📸 Use this moment**, or **📁 Choose a picture** instead. On your own videos, **🖼️ Thumbnail** changes it any time.
- **Boys and girls 🚻:** everyone picks **💙 Boy** or **💗 Girl** and does a face scan, before they can use Yakimma (accounts made before this pick the next time they come). The scan happens on the device and only sends what it guessed, never the picture. When it agrees, that's their gender. When it doesn't, or it can't tell, or they can't do a scan, they wait on a **Waiting for the creator** screen, and the creator gets a 🔔 notification and chooses Boy or Girl for them on the Safety page (**🚻 Gender checks**). The creator just picks, with no scan. Only posts are split: making a post, you have to choose who it's for, **💙 Boys**, **💗 Girls** or **💜 Both**. Boys see posts for boys and for both, girls see posts for girls and for both, and guests only see posts for both. Everyone sees their own posts, and the creator sees every post so they can delete ones in the wrong zone. The label on a post shows who it's for; the person who posted it, and the creator, can tap it to change it. Friends, chats and groups are for everyone (group posts are always for the whole group), and posts from before this are for both. It's the same in every zone (the normal site, the kiddy zone and the 18+ zone). Boys are blue and girls are pink.
- **Suspensions ⛔:** only the creator can suspend someone, for any number of days, with a reason they'll see: from the Reports page (the person who posted it, or someone who made a false report), from someone's profile, or on the Safety page. A suspended account can't do anything (or sign in) until the suspension is over; it sees how long it lasts and why, and can still read the rules. The creator can lift a suspension early, and can never be suspended.
- **Safety 🛡️:** a page under Reports that only the creator sees, with every friend chat and group chat (read-only), every comment, every private and unlisted post, and who's suspended. Type a username to see only what that person was part of. The Rules page tells everyone the creator can see these, to keep Yakimma safe.
- **Rules 📜:** the Rules page is under Settings (the list is `RULES` in `public/app.js`). Right after someone makes an account, a **Read The Rules!!** pop-up sends them there.
- **Comments 💬:** tap **💬 Comment** on any post you can see to read its comments and write one. Tap **😊 React** to react to a comment with an emoji, or **↩️ Reply** to answer it: replies show under the comment, and replying to a reply starts it with @their username. Whoever wrote the comment gets a 🔔 notification. A comment or reply can be deleted by the person who wrote it, the person whose post it is, or the creator, and deleting a comment deletes its replies too.
- **Tags:** write @ and someone's username (like @Creator) in a post, a comment or a chat message to tag them. The tag becomes a link to their profile, and they get a notification if they can see that post or chat. Someone tagged in a group they're not in still gets the notification: tapping it shows that group's chat with only the messages they were tagged in, and every other message blurred (the server never sends what those say or who sent them).
- **Notifications 🔔:** the bell at the top shows how many new notifications you have. You get one when someone tags you, sends you a friend request, accepts yours, follows you, likes or comments on your post, reacts to your message or comment, or makes you their assistant. Tap one to go to it. Opening the bell marks them all as seen. Nothing comes from people you blocked, or who blocked you.
- **Assistant 🤝:** open the profile of a friend you trust and tap **🤝 Make my assistant**. Everyone can see it on your profile ("Assistant") and on theirs ("Assistant to"). You can have one assistant, and take them off any time. Only friends can be assistants, nobody can choose one in the kiddy zone, and when two people stop being friends (or one blocks the other) the assistant goes.
- **Nicknames 🏷️:** on a friend's profile, tap **🏷️ Nickname** to give them a nickname only you can see. It shows instead of their name everywhere on Yakimma, just for you, and you can search for them by it. It stops showing if you stop being friends (and comes back if you're friends again). Nobody can be nicknamed after the creator.
- **Playlist 🎶:** signed in, tap **➕ Playlist** on any post (yours or someone else's) to put it in **🎶 My playlist** on your Profile, to see it again later. Tap **✓ In playlist** to take it out. The playlist shows the newest added first (the first 3, then **Show all**), up to 200 posts. Posts you can't see any more drop out of it: deleted, made private, from someone blocked, in a group you left, or from another zone.
- **Passwords:** every password box has an eye button. The eye is open while the password is hidden as dots (tap to show it) and closed while it's shown.
- **Don't know your password?** The sign-in box has two other ways in:
  - **📧 Email + Google:** type the email on your account, then sign in to Google with it. This only works after you've added your email with **📧 Email** on your Profile (Yakimma emails a 6-digit code to check it's yours). Each email can only be on one account, and nobody else sees it. It needs a Google sign-in client (see **Sign in with Google** below).
  - **📲 Quick sign in:** the new device shows an 8-letter code. On a device that's already signed in, type it in the **📲 Quick sign in** box on your Profile (above My collection) and press **✅ Confirm**, and the new device is signed in to your account within a couple of seconds. The old device stays signed in. Codes work for 5 minutes and only once, and 10 wrong codes means a 15-minute wait.
- **Staying signed in:** sign-ins are saved in the database, so refreshing, closing the website or the server restarting never signs you out. Each visit keeps you signed in for another year (`SESSION_DAYS` in `server.js`).
- **Changing your username:** on your Profile, **✏️ Change Username** offers **Change Capitalization?** (free, like cutie to Cutie) or **Change Whole Username?** (50 MC, `USERNAME_COST` in `server.js`). You can't copy someone else's username, and the admins' usernames can only change their capital letters.

- **Songs 🎵:** in Settings, pick Default (No Song), Gnarlyyy, GameBoy, Dolly Babe, I Know You Belongg, How You Like That?, Love Funk, That's How Life Goes, Trending or Quran Gojol to play on a loop on every page. Tap the 👀 followers or ➡️ following count, on your Profile or anyone's, to see who they are and open their profiles. ## Before you run it

Install Node.js 20 or newer. The live server runs Node 20.20.2, and `.nvmrc` pins that version so `nvm use` matches it. Then, in this folder:

```bash
npm install
npm start
```

Open http://localhost:3000

New accounts start with 0 MC. To test the Nail Salon locally, enter a code that gives LoveCards on the Codes page, then unlock Nails in the Shop. You'll also need a friend: make a second account (for example in a private window) and send a friend request.

The codes live in `server.js`, so anyone who can read the source code can see them. Keep the GitLab repository private.

## Deploying to Render

`render.yaml` sets up a Node **Web Service** with a 5 GB persistent disk mounted at `/var/data`. The database and uploads are stored there, so they survive deploys and restarts.

1. Push this folder to a GitHub or GitLab repository.
2. In Render, choose **New → Blueprint** and pick the repository.
3. Approve the plan. Render creates the service and disk, and generates `SESSION_SECRET` for you.
4. For the 18+ zone, the codes are emailed over SMTP. In the service's **Environment** tab, add `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_USER` and `SMTP_PASS`. For a cPanel email account, cPanel lists them under **Email Accounts → Connect Devices**: the outgoing server, port 465, the whole email address, and its password. Codes are sent from `SMTP_USER`, or from `MAIL_FROM` if you set it. Without these settings, nobody can turn on 18+ on the live site. On your own computer, run `npm run email-setup` to save them in the `.env` file (it's never committed) and check they work. Until they're filled in, the code is shown on screen.

A disk needs a paid instance (Starter or higher). It also limits the service to one instance, and each deploy has a few seconds of downtime. Logins are kept in memory, so everyone is signed out whenever the service restarts.

Locally nothing changes: without `DATA_DIR` and `UPLOAD_DIR` set, the app uses the `data/` and `uploads/` folders here.

## Sign in with Google

**📧 Email + Google** needs a Google sign-in client. Until it's set up, the sign-in box says it isn't ready yet. To set it up:

1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com, make a project (any name, like Yakimma), and open **APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen**. Choose **External**, fill in the app name and your email, and publish it.
2. Open **APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID**, and choose **Web application**.
3. Under **Authorized redirect URIs**, add `http://localhost:3000/api/auth/google/callback`, and the same for the live site (`https://<your site>/api/auth/google/callback`).
4. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret** into `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`: in the `.env` file on your own computer, and in Render's **Environment** tab for the live site. Then restart Yakimma.

## Important limitations before public launch

This is a **local MVP**, not a deployed social network. SQLite and local upload files are fine for development, but not enough for a reliable public site with many users. Before launch, have an experienced developer configure:

1. PostgreSQL or another managed production database.
2. Private object storage/CDN for uploads (for example, Cloudflare R2 or an equivalent).
3. A strong `SESSION_SECRET` environment variable and HTTPS.
4. Production session storage (not Express's default in-memory store).
5. Upload quotas, video transcoding, moderation/reporting, blocking, deletion, backup and recovery.
6. Age-appropriate privacy/safety policies and legal review, especially because social platforms can attract young users. The 18+ face scan only estimates age from the camera in the browser. It can be off by years, a photo of a grown-up can fool it, and someone who knows how could skip it. A real age check needs a proper age-verification service.
7. Friend-request safety: blocking, reporting, and notifications. Friends and the Nail Salon work, but pages refresh when you open them rather than updating live.
8. Real viewing controls. Speed/effects should apply to the actual video player; the initial UI includes speed control and simple viewing effects.

Do not commit real passwords, secrets, private user data, or uploaded media to source control. Do not expose this local development server directly to the public internet.

## Currency / anti-abuse notes

The server—not the browser—calculates balances and purchase costs. A post owner cannot earn MC by liking their own post. Each liker can reward a given post only once, even if they unlike and like again. A production version should also add spam detection, bot/fake-account controls and clear moderation rules.
